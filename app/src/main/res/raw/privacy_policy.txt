Music and recordings
DJ IMAN accesses audio that you allow it to read so you can browse, analyse, mix and play music. Track names, artist information, beat grids, cue points, pad sounds and settings are processed and stored on your device. The app's music and mix-recording features do not upload your songs or recorded mixes to our subscription service. Mix recording captures the app's master output, not the phone microphone.
Recordings remain in the app until you delete them or clear its storage. Use the recorder's DELETE button to remove an in-app recording. Copies you export using Android's save dialog are stored at the destination you choose; deleting the in-app copy does not delete those exported copies. If you select cloud storage or share a recording through another app, that provider's privacy terms apply.
Subscriptions and payment
In the private test build, opening-code verification happens locally and no subscription payment is taken. In the Google Play subscription build, Google processes the payment. DJ IMAN does not receive your full payment-card details or Google password.
For purchase verification, the app sends a Google Play purchase token, product and package identifiers, a random installation identifier and a one-use request identifier to our verification service over HTTPS. The service asks Google to confirm subscription status, records the subscription state and expiry, and issues a time-limited signed access receipt. This information is used to provide paid access, restore purchases, prevent purchase reuse and handle subscription changes. It is not used by our subscription service for advertising.
The service stores purchase tokens encrypted, an installation-identifier hash, subscription status, expiry and verification timestamps. Active records are retained while needed to provide access. The supplied service removes records more than 90 days after both the subscription expiry and the last verification, on a later verification or a scheduled cleanup. Ask the support contact below to request earlier deletion where applicable. Deleting verification data does not cancel a Google Play subscription; restoring a valid purchase creates the necessary verification record again. Network infrastructure necessarily handles IP addresses to deliver requests; hosting and operational log retention must be confirmed for the production service.

Your choices and deletion
Allow or revoke music access in Android Settings → Apps → DJ IMAN → Permissions.
Delete individual recordings from the recorder panel, and separately delete exported files where you saved them.
Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall it, to remove its local settings, pad data, access receipt and private recordings. Export wanted recordings first. Original music files and exported recordings outside the app are not removed by this action.
Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Uninstalling, deleting local data or revoking music access does not cancel recurring payments.
DJ IMAN does not currently create a separate DJ IMAN user account. Purchase restoration uses the Google Play account on your device. Contact support for verification-data enquiries.
Security and changes
Subscription requests use HTTPS. Signing credentials and Google service credentials belong only on the verification server. The app verifies signed access receipts and does not treat pending or failed payments as paid access. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. This policy will be updated when data practices change.
